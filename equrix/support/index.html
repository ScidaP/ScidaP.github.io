Equrix Support

Need help with gameplay, sign-in, purchases, privacy choices, or your account? Contact Equrix support and include the app version shown at the bottom of Settings, your device model, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or full payment information.

Email Equrix Support

Purchases

Use Settings → Restore Purchases after reinstalling Equrix or changing devices. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

Account deletion

You can initiate deletion from Settings → Delete account or use the web deletion page.
